Services

From ground data to structure

Soil investigation, testing, structural design and water engineering in one office, so site data stays connected to design decisions.

01

Geotechnical & foundations

“What foundation is safe and economical on this site?”

  • Boreholes, soil investigation and foundation reports
  • Shallow, deep and pile foundation design
  • Foundation recommendations based on test results

Record: soil and foundation reports from 2011 to 2025 across many governorates.

02

Engineering support for contractors

“We need a dewatering or excavation-support design to start digging.”

  • Dewatering design
  • Excavation-support (shoring) design
  • Method statements and adjacent-structure safety reports

Record: sewerage projects in Dakahlia, Qalyubia, Sharqia and Cairo, 2021–2023.

03

Field & laboratory testing

“Is the fill, concrete or steel on site acceptable?”

  • Compaction and plate load tests
  • Pile load and well tests
  • Concrete QC, tensile tests, mix design

Record: schools, hospitals, treatment plants and housing projects in the Delta and Canal region.

04

Structural design & supervision

“Design and supervise our building.”

  • Reinforced concrete buildings up to basement, ground and 11 floors
  • Educational, healthcare, industrial and public buildings
  • Raft and pile foundations

Record: residential towers in Mansoura and Mit Ghamr, university buildings, hospitals.

05

Water, wastewater & irrigation

“Structures for our water project.”

  • Structural and geotechnical engineering for water and wastewater projects
  • Elevated water tanks, 200–1,000 m³
  • Bridges (30–90 t), siphons, weirs, culverts

Record: Menoufia, Kafr El-Sheikh, Beheira, Sinai, Fayoum, Minya.

06

Inspection & design review

“Is this building, or this design, safe?”

  • Structural condition reports for existing buildings
  • Concrete assessment, repair and strengthening design
  • Independent review of structural designs

Record: design review office for the decennial liability insurance pool, 2005–2021.

We design to the Egyptian Code; other codes on request, coordinated with the locally licensed engineer of record. Deliverables in Arabic and English. We also prepare integrated architectural and structural packages, tender documents and time schedules for public buildings.
