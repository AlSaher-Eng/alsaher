Who we are

An academic foundation, proven in the field.

Al Saher Engineering Consultancy was founded in 1996 by Prof. Dr. Saher Rafaat El-Khoraiby, former Dean of the Faculty of Engineering, Tanta University, and Prof. Dr. Bakenaz Zeidan, on one principle: engineering integrity above all else.

Residential towers & villas Commercial & mixed-use Industrial facilities Hospitals & healthcare Educational buildings Water & wastewater Bridges & irrigation structures Mosques & institutional

Our story

1996
Founded in Mansoura by Prof. Dr. Saher El-Khoraiby and Prof. Dr. Bakenaz Zeidan: geotechnical and structural engineering.
2000s
Expansion into water and wastewater projects for the Egyptian Authority for Drinking Water & Sanitary Drainage.
2005–2021
Design review office for the Egyptian Decennial Liability Insurance Pool for construction works.
2021
Prof. Dr. Bakenaz Zeidan becomes CEO; Dr. Raghda El-Khoraiby becomes General Director.
Services

Engineering decisions based on tested data

Soil investigation, field and laboratory testing, structural design and water infrastructure in one office, so site data stays connected to design decisions.

01

Geotechnical & Foundations

  • Shallow and deep foundation and pile design
  • Dewatering and excavation-support design for contractors
02

Field & Laboratory Testing

  • Compaction and plate load tests
  • Pile load tests, well and dewatering tests
  • Concrete quality control, rebar tensile tests and concrete mix design

Structural Design & Supervision

  • Reinforced concrete buildings: residential towers up to basement, ground and 11 floors
  • Public, educational, healthcare and industrial buildings
  • Construction supervision

Water, Wastewater & Irrigation

  • Elevated water tanks (200–1,000 m³)
  • Bridges (30–90 t), siphons, weirs and culverts
05

Inspection & Design Review

  • Structural condition and safety reports

Integrated Packages & Support

Codes & documentation

Egyptian Code (ECP)

We design to the Egyptian Code. Other codes on request, coordinated with the locally licensed engineer of record. Deliverables in Arabic and English. A soil and concrete laboratory supports foundation and quality decisions.
