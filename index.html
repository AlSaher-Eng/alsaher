Who we are

An academic foundation, proven in the field.

Al Saher Engineering Consultancy was founded in 1996 by Prof. Dr. Saher Rafaat El-Khoraiby, former Dean of the Faculty of Engineering, Tanta University, on one principle: engineering integrity above all else.
